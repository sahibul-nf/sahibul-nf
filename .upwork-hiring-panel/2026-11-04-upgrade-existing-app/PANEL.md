## Shape check against winning patterns
- Line one names the client's kind of app: partly. It names the job's work, not an app built.
- Line two: one hard credential with a number: missing.
- Clickable proof list (store links first): missing. Only sahibul.dev.
- Every ask answered: partly, with no counts and no links.
- Length: about 360 words. The rule is 60 to 180 before links.

## Numbered problems, worst first
1. **Unverified store claim.** "(and other client/store apps I have published)" and "I have also shipped hiQuran and Nourdaily to the stores." The job requires personally published apps on both stores. Nothing here is checkable. Flagged by Skeptic, Hiring, Competitor, Veteran.
2. **No store links.** The job asks for 2 to 3 links to apps you published. The proposal gives only sahibul.dev. Flagged by Hiring, Competitor, Veteran.
3. **Rate mismatch.** Card $20/hr, bid and letter $25/hr. Flagged by Budget, Competitor, Skeptic, Veteran.
4. **Generic line.** "I work on current stable Flutter/Dart on every project; I will match yours after the first clone." Flagged by Technical Lead, Competitor, Budget.
5. **Store-count answers unclear.** Answers 2, 3 and 6 repeat the same app list with no counts. Flagged by Technical Lead, Veteran.
6. **No estimate or date.** "$25/hr" with no hours, no finish date, and "possibly ongoing" unpriced. No plan for a rejected build. Flagged by Budget, Competitor.
7. **Signing ownership unanswered.** No mention of who holds the Android upload keystore and the Apple distribution certificate. Flagged by Technical Lead (as the question they should have asked).
8. **No Flutter/Dart version.** The job asks for it. Flagged by Skeptic, Hiring.
9. **Template tell.** "That is the work I already do on client apps" and the job restated in line one. Flagged by Veteran.
10. **Length.** About 360 words. Flagged by Veteran.
